      1My Plans

      You can keep up to three plans and switch between them to compare different paths. Rename a plan, or copy one plan to another to use as a starting point.

      At a glance (compare your plans)

      2Prior Credit & Placement

      Enter credit and placement the student brings into the Upper School. These feed the prerequisite checks and the credit tracker below. Middle School course names follow the Middle School Scheduling & Curriculum Guide 2026–2027.

      From Woodward Middle School

      Transfer credit (coursework from another school in grades 9–12, including approved summer forward credit)

      If the course matches a Woodward course, choose it so prerequisites are satisfied (for example Biology or Algebra 2). Otherwise pick a subject area and enter credits. Transfer work appears as TR- on the transcript and is not included in WA GPAs.

      3Four-Year Plan

      Year-long courses are chosen in the Fall / Full Year dropdown and automatically fill both semesters. For semester courses, choose a Fall course and a Spring course. Items marked ⚑ approval need a teacher’s or department chair’s approval, an audition or an application. Courses whose prerequisites are not yet met are greyed out at the end of each list.

      4Cocurricular Activities

      Record the clubs, teams, arts groups and service programs the student is involved in now, or would like to explore in the future (from the Upper School Cocurricular Guide).

      +My Notes (for this plan — optional)

      Please don’t type your full name, address or other personal details here. Notes are included when you print or download this plan.

      5Save, Print & Share

      Save my plans

      Downloads one file with all three plans so you can reopen them later, on this or another device.

      Share with my counselor

      Downloads the plan you are viewing (Plan A) as a file your counselor can import into their planner.

      Print or save a PDF

      Opens a clean printout of the plan you are viewing. Choose Save as PDF as the printer to make a PDF.

      Open a saved file

      Open a file you downloaded earlier — either “all my plans” or a single plan.

      Your plans are stored only in this browser on this device. On a shared or school computer, download your plans and then clear them.
